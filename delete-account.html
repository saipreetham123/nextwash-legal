Delete your NextWash account

Applies to the NextWash mobile app, published by NextWash.

You can ask us to delete your NextWash account and the personal data associated with it at any time. There is no charge, and you do not need to give a reason.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@nextwash.com with the subject line “Delete my NextWash account”.
  2. Include the mobile number registered on your NextWash account, so we can identify it.
  3. We will verify that the request comes from the account holder. We may send a one-time code to the registered number to confirm this.
  4. Once verified, we delete the account and confirm by email. We aim to complete this within 30 days of a verified request.
You can also call +91 99989 83765, or use Help & Support inside the app, and ask for your account to be deleted.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted permanentlyKept, and why
  • Your profile: name, email address, mobile number and profile picture
  • All saved pickup and delivery addresses, including map coordinates
  • Your cart and any saved preferences
  • Device push-notification tokens
  • Subscription records and any remaining wallet credit
  • Your sign-in identity, so the account can no longer be used
  • Order and payment records required for tax, accounting and audit purposes under applicable Indian law. These are retained for as long as that law requires and are then deleted.
  • Retained records are separated from your profile so they no longer identify you as a NextWash user, wherever that is possible.

Any unused wallet credit is forfeited when the account is deleted. If you have credit you want to use, place an order before requesting deletion.

Deleting only some of your data

If you would rather keep your account but remove specific information, you can edit your profile and delete saved addresses directly in the app, or email us at the address above describing what you would like removed.
