Privacy Policy

NextWash · Last updated 16 September 2026

This policy explains what NextWash ("we", "us") collects when you use the NextWash mobile application, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We only collect what the service needs in order to pick up, clean and return your clothes.

NextWash currently operates in Hyderabad, India, and this policy is provided under Indian law.

Information we collect

DataWhy we need it
Mobile numberTo create your account and sign you in. We verify it by one-time password (OTP).
Name and email addressTo identify your orders and send order confirmations and invoices.
Pickup and delivery addresses, including map coordinatesSo our rider can collect and return your order, and so we can confirm your area is serviceable.
Approximate or precise device locationOnly when you choose to detect your location while adding an address. You can type an address instead.
Photos or camera imagesOnly if you choose to set a profile picture. We do not access your gallery otherwise.
Order details and historyTo process your bookings, show your order status and provide support.
Payment confirmation dataTo confirm an order has been paid. See "Payments" below.
Device push tokenTo notify you about your order status.
Diagnostic and error informationTo detect failures such as sign-in problems so we can fix them.

Payments

Card, UPI, netbanking and wallet payments are processed by our payment provider, Razorpay. Your full card or bank details are entered in Razorpay's own secure checkout and are never collected by, sent to, or stored on NextWash servers. We retain only the payment reference and status needed to confirm your order.

How we use your information

  • To provide the service: scheduling pickups, processing orders and arranging delivery.
  • To keep you informed about your order status.
  • To provide customer support when you contact us.
  • To operate subscription plans, coupons and referrals if you use them.
  • To keep the app secure and diagnose technical problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run NextWash, and only to the extent they need it:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and backend services.
  • Google Firebase — phone-number verification and push notifications.
  • Google Maps Platform — maps, address search and converting addresses to locations.
  • Razorpay — payment processing.
  • Our delivery staff — receive the name, address and phone number needed to complete your pickup or delivery.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is active, and afterwards only where needed for legal, tax or accounting purposes. Diagnostic logs are kept for a short period and then deleted.

Your choices

  • Location: you can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings and still use the app by entering addresses manually.
  • Camera and photos: optional, and only used for a profile picture.
  • Notifications: can be turned off in your device settings.
  • Access, correction and deletion: you can edit your profile and addresses in the app. To request a copy of your data or deletion of your account, contact us using the details below and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

NextWash is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access controls that restrict each customer's records to that customer and to authorised NextWash staff. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app.
